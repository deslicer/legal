## DESLICER AI

### 1. Service Description

Deslicer AI is a multi-agent artificial intelligence system designed to manage, analyze, and optimize data, configurations, and assets related to log management and observability platforms and connected support services. Deslicer AI provides the following capabilities:

- **Asset and Configuration Discovery:** Automated discovery and normalization of configurations, assets, and dependencies across log management and observability platforms.

- **Configuration Intelligence:** Analysis of configuration patterns, detection of deviations from baselines, and identification of optimization opportunities.

- **Policy Validation:** Assessment of configurations against defined policies, industry best practices, and compliance requirements.

- **Operational Insights:** Generation of insights and recommendations for operational improvements, cost optimization, and risk mitigation.

- **Automation Support:** Semi-automated or fully automated generation of change proposals for configuration optimization.

Deslicer AI utilizes generative artificial intelligence (AI) technologies, including large language models (LLMs), to provide intelligent analysis and recommendations. The service is delivered as a SaaS offering and may include supporting On-Premises Software components where specified in the Order.

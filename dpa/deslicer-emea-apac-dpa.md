## DESLICER DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (EMEA & APAC)

<img src="../media/logo.png" alt="Deslicer logo" width="300" />

Last Updated: January 7, 2026

This Data Processing Agreement (**"DPA"**) forms part of the Deslicer General Terms (the **"General Terms"**) or other master agreement between Deslicer AB (**"Deslicer"**) and the entity identified as customer in the applicable Order (**"Customer"**) (together, the **"Agreement"**).

This DPA reflects the parties’ agreement with respect to the Processing of Personal Data in connection with the Offerings provided by Deslicer to Customer in the EMEA and APAC regions.

If there is any conflict between this DPA and the Agreement, this DPA will prevail to the extent of the conflict with respect to the Processing of Personal Data.

---

### 1. Roles of the Parties and Scope

1.1 **Roles.** For the purposes of applicable Data Protection Laws, including the GDPR, Customer is the **Data Controller** and Deslicer is the **Data Processor** in respect of any Personal Data Processed as part of the Offerings.

1.2 **Scope of Processing.** This DPA applies solely to Deslicer’s Processing of Personal Data on behalf of Customer as necessary to provide, support, secure and improve the Offerings under the Agreement, including:

- Deslicer AI;
- Deslicer DevOps; and
- Deslicer Automation Platform,

in each case only to the extent Deslicer Processes Personal Data as a Data Processor.

1.3 **Customer Instructions.** Deslicer will Process Personal Data only:

- on documented instructions from Customer as set out in the Agreement, this DPA and the Documentation, and
- as further documented in Customer’s configuration and use of the Offerings,

unless Deslicer is required to Process Personal Data by applicable law, in which case Deslicer will inform Customer of that legal requirement before Processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

1.4 **Customer Responsibility for Instructions.** Customer is responsible for ensuring that its instructions (including configuration and use of the Offerings) comply with applicable Data Protection Laws. Deslicer will notify Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes applicable Data Protection Laws, but is not responsible for determining whether Customer’s instructions comply with such laws.

---

### 2. Types of Personal Data and Data Subjects

2.1 **Categories of Data Subjects.** Personal Data Processed by Deslicer on behalf of Customer may concern the following categories of Data Subjects:

- Customer’s employees, contractors and other users authorized to use the Offerings;
- employees, contractors and other users of Customer’s managed systems and environments;
- individuals whose Personal Data appears in configurations, logs, telemetry data or other operational data that Customer ingests into or exposes via the Offerings; and
- any other individuals whose Personal Data is Processed at Customer’s direction through the Offerings.

2.2 **Categories of Personal Data.** Depending on the Offerings used and Customer’s configuration, the Personal Data may include:

- contact and identification data (such as names, business email addresses, user IDs, role information);
- technical identifiers and usage data (such as IP addresses, device identifiers, system account IDs, audit log entries, configuration metadata containing user identifiers);
- content contained in configuration assets, comments or annotations, and change descriptions submitted by users; and
- any other Personal Data that Customer chooses to include in inputs, configurations, logs, telemetry or other data sources Processed by the Offerings.

2.3 **Special Categories.** The Offerings are not intended for Processing special categories of Personal Data (sensitive data) as defined in the GDPR or equivalent categories under other Data Protection Laws. If Customer chooses to include such data, Customer is responsible for ensuring that appropriate legal grounds and safeguards apply. Deslicer will treat such data as Personal Data and Process it in accordance with this DPA.

---

### 3. Duration of Processing

3.1 **Processing Duration.** Deslicer will Process Personal Data for the duration of the Agreement and any applicable Order, and thereafter as required or permitted under section 9 (Return and Deletion) of this DPA or applicable law.

---

### 4. Deslicer’s Obligations as Processor

4.1 **Processing on Customer’s Behalf.** Deslicer will Process Personal Data solely for the following purposes:

- providing, operating, maintaining and supporting the Offerings;
- performing its obligations and exercising its rights under the Agreement;
- ensuring the security and integrity of the Offerings;
- improving and developing the Offerings, where permitted under the Agreement and this DPA (including with respect to AI Service Data as described in the Deslicer AI Specific Terms); and
- complying with applicable law.

4.2 **Confidentiality.** Deslicer will ensure that persons authorized to Process Personal Data are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations, whether contractual or statutory.

4.3 **Security Measures.** Deslicer will implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure or access, as described in the Security section of the General Terms and any applicable Security Exhibits. Customer acknowledges that such measures are subject to technical progress and development and that Deslicer may update them from time to time, provided that such updates do not materially decrease the overall level of protection for Personal Data.

4.4 **Subprocessing.** Customer authorizes Deslicer to appoint Subprocessors to Process Personal Data on Deslicer’s behalf as necessary to provide the Offerings. Deslicer will:

- ensure that Subprocessors are bound by written agreements imposing data protection obligations no less protective than those set out in this DPA; and
- remain responsible to Customer for the performance of Subprocessors’ obligations to the same extent Deslicer would be responsible if performing the services directly.

Deslicer will make available a current list of Subprocessors (including AI-related Subprocessors where applicable) at a URL notified to Customer (for example, the URL referenced in the General Terms or the Deslicer AI Specific Terms). Where required by applicable Data Protection Laws, Deslicer will provide advance notice of additions or replacements of Subprocessors, and Customer may object on reasonable grounds relating to data protection within the period stated in that notice. If Customer objects and the parties cannot resolve the objection within a reasonable period, Customer may, as its sole and exclusive remedy, terminate the affected Offering by providing written notice before the change takes effect and receive a pro rata refund of any prepaid, unused Fees for the terminated portion of the Term.

4.5 **International Transfers.** Where Deslicer or its Subprocessors Process Personal Data outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or other jurisdiction that imposes data transfer restrictions, Deslicer will ensure that such transfers are made in compliance with applicable Data Protection Laws, such as by:

- implementing appropriate safeguards under Article 46 GDPR (including, where applicable, the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or UK International Data Transfer Addendum); or
- relying on an adequacy decision or other applicable transfer mechanism.

Where Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), UK International Data Transfer Addendum (UK IDTA), or similar transfer mechanisms are required, Deslicer will execute such mechanisms with Customer under a separate agreement or as an addendum to this DPA. Upon Customer's request, Deslicer will provide information about the applicable transfer mechanisms and enter into the required data transfer agreements.

Standard Contractual Clauses, UK IDTA, and other transfer mechanism templates are available at [https://github.com/deslicer/legal/tree/main/dpa/addenda](https://github.com/deslicer/legal/tree/main/dpa/addenda) for execution upon Customer's request. Details of Deslicer's current data transfer mechanisms and Processing locations may be requested by contacting Deslicer at [legal@deslicer.com](mailto:legal@deslicer.com). The subprocessor list is maintained at [https://github.com/deslicer/legal/blob/main/privacy/subprocessors.md](https://github.com/deslicer/legal/blob/main/privacy/subprocessors.md).

4.6 **Assistance with Data Subject Requests.** Taking into account the nature of the Processing, Deslicer will assist Customer by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as possible, in fulfilling Customer’s obligations to respond to Data Subject requests under Data Protection Laws (including requests for access, rectification, restriction, portability or erasure). To the extent permitted by law, Customer will be responsible for any costs reasonably incurred by Deslicer in providing such assistance that goes beyond standard functionality of the Offerings.

4.7 **Assistance with Compliance.** Deslicer will provide reasonable assistance to Customer with:

- Customer’s obligations relating to security of Processing, Personal Data Breaches, data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with supervisory authorities, taking into account the nature of Processing and the information available to Deslicer; and
- Customer’s responses to inquiries or investigations by supervisory authorities relating to Deslicer’s Processing of Personal Data on Customer’s behalf.

4.8 **Personal Data Breach Notification.** In the event of a Personal Data Breach affecting Personal Data Processed by Deslicer on behalf of Customer, Deslicer will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of the breach. Such notification may be provided via email or through Customer’s designated account contacts. Deslicer will provide information reasonably available to it about the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of affected Data Subjects and Personal Data records, and the measures taken or proposed to address the breach, in accordance with Data Protection Laws. Customer is responsible for complying with any legal notification obligations applicable to Customer, including notifying supervisory authorities and Data Subjects.

4.9 **Records and Audit.** Deslicer will maintain records of Processing activities performed on behalf of Customer to the extent required by Data Protection Laws. Upon Customer’s written request no more than once in any twelve (12) month period (unless required more frequently by a competent supervisory authority), Deslicer will:

- make available to Customer information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA; and
- allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by Customer or an independent auditor mandated by Customer, subject to reasonable advance notice, scope, duration, and security and confidentiality requirements.

Audits will be conducted during normal business hours and in a manner that does not unreasonably interfere with Deslicer’s business operations. Customer is responsible for all costs associated with any such audit, including Deslicer’s reasonable internal costs. Deslicer may satisfy its audit obligations by providing existing third-party audit reports, certifications, or summaries that demonstrate the effectiveness of its technical and organizational measures, provided that such reports reasonably address the relevant requirements of this DPA.

### 5. Customer’s Obligations

5.1 **Lawful Basis and Transparency.** Customer is responsible for:

- ensuring that there is a valid legal basis under Data Protection Laws for the Processing of Personal Data in connection with the Offerings;
- providing all required notices to Data Subjects; and
- obtaining any necessary consents or authorizations.

5.2 **Data Minimization.** Customer will not provide Deslicer with Personal Data beyond what is reasonably necessary to use the Offerings for the permitted purposes. Customer will not use the Offerings to Process special categories of Personal Data or Personal Data relating to criminal convictions and offences unless the Offerings are specifically designed for such use and this has been expressly agreed in writing.

Customer acknowledges that, despite data minimization efforts, AI-powered and telemetry-driven services may incidentally capture special categories of Personal Data through logs, configurations, or user-generated content. Where such incidental capture occurs, Deslicer will Process such data in accordance with this DPA and will implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the data against unauthorized disclosure. Customer remains responsible, as Data Controller, for establishing a lawful basis for the Processing of special categories of Personal Data under both Article 6 (lawful basis for processing) and Article 9 (separate condition for special categories of data) of the GDPR (or equivalent provisions of other Data Protection Laws). Customer must ensure that both a general lawful basis under Article 6 and a specific condition under Article 9 are met before Processing special categories of Personal Data through the Offerings. Deslicer's processor-level obligations under this DPA, including security, confidentiality, and data subject assistance, apply fully to all Personal Data Processed under this DPA, including special categories of Personal Data.

5.3 **Configuration and Security Controls.** Customer is responsible for:

- configuring and using the Offerings in accordance with the Agreement, this DPA and the Documentation;
- implementing appropriate access controls, user roles and approval workflows;
- protecting authentication credentials, keys, certificates and tokens; and
- implementing appropriate security measures within Customer’s own systems and environments, including systems that integrate with the Offerings.

---

### 6. Use of Processors for AI and Analytics

6.1 **AI Service Data.** Where Customer uses Deslicer AI or other AI-powered Offerings, the treatment of AI Service Data (including Inputs and Outputs) is governed by the Deslicer AI Specific Terms and this DPA. To the extent AI Service Data includes Personal Data:

- Deslicer Processes such Personal Data solely as Customer's Processor to provide, operate, maintain, support and secure the Offerings in accordance with the Agreement and this DPA, and not to train AI models or build general-purpose AI services; and
- Deslicer contractually requires its AI-related Subprocessors not to use Customer Content or Personal Data contained in AI Service Data as training data for their own models or services.

Where Deslicer processes aggregated, de-identified service analytics that no longer constitute Personal Data, such processing falls outside the scope of this DPA and the GDPR's controller-processor framework, and is governed by the confidentiality and usage provisions of the Agreement. Such analytics do not contain Customer Content or Personal Data. Deslicer implements appropriate safeguards when generating and using such analytics, including data minimization, aggregation and de-identification, and maintains technical and organizational measures to mitigate the risk of unintended disclosure or memorization.

### 7. Subprocessing in EMEA and APAC

7.1 **Regional Subprocessors.** Without prejudice to section 4.4, Deslicer may engage Subprocessors located in EMEA, APAC and other regions, provided that appropriate data transfer mechanisms are in place as described in section 4.5.

7.2 **Subprocessor List and Notification.** Deslicer will maintain an up-to-date list of Subprocessors involved in the Processing of Personal Data under this DPA and make it available to Customer upon request or via a public URL notified to Customer. Where required by Data Protection Laws, Deslicer will provide advance notice of changes to this list and follow the objection and termination process described in section 4.4.

---

### 8. Data Protection Impact Assessments and Consultation

8.1 **DPIA Assistance.** To the extent the Processing of Personal Data by Deslicer is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, and Customer is required to carry out a data protection impact assessment (DPIA), Deslicer will provide reasonable assistance and information to Customer to help Customer conduct such DPIA, taking into account the nature of the Processing and the information available to Deslicer.

8.2 **Prior Consultation.** Where Customer is required to consult with a supervisory authority prior to Processing, Deslicer will provide reasonable assistance to Customer, to the extent relating to Deslicer’s Processing on Customer’s behalf and to the extent such assistance is required under Data Protection Laws.

---

### 9. Return and Deletion of Personal Data

9.1 **Data at the End of the Term.** Upon termination or expiry of the Agreement or an applicable Order, Deslicer will, at Customer’s choice and subject to technical feasibility:

- delete Personal Data Processed on behalf of Customer; or
- return such Personal Data to Customer,

except to the extent Deslicer is required by applicable law to retain some or all of the Personal Data or where Deslicer has a lawful basis to retain certain Personal Data as an independent controller (for example, for audit, compliance or dispute resolution purposes).

9.2 **Backups and Logs.** Deletion obligations may be satisfied through standard decommissioning processes, which may render Personal Data unrecoverable rather than physically erased from all backup systems. Deslicer may retain Personal Data in system logs and backups for a limited period consistent with its backup retention policies, after which the data will be overwritten or deleted in the ordinary course of business.

---

### 10. Liability

10.1 **Liability Cap.** The limitations and exclusions of liability in section 18 of the General Terms apply to this DPA and to any claims arising out of or in connection with the Processing of Personal Data under or in connection with the Agreement and this DPA.

10.2 **Allocation of Responsibility.** Each party is responsible for any damages it causes by Processing in breach of Data Protection Laws. Where both parties are responsible for the same damage and cannot be distinctly apportioned, the allocation of responsibility between the parties will be determined in accordance with applicable law and the General Terms.

---

### 11. Miscellaneous

11.1 **Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** This DPA is governed by the same law that governs the Agreement, and any disputes arising out of or in connection with this DPA will be subject to the same dispute resolution provisions as set out in the Agreement.

11.2 **Amendments.** Deslicer may update this DPA from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or Deslicer’s Processing practices, provided that such updates do not materially reduce the protections afforded to Personal Data. Material changes will be communicated to Customer in accordance with the notice provisions of the Agreement.

11.3 **Order of Precedence.** In case of any conflict between this DPA and other terms in the Agreement relating to data protection, this DPA will prevail to the extent of the conflict. In case of conflict between this DPA and Standard Contractual Clauses or other data transfer mechanisms entered into between the parties, the latter will prevail to the extent they are required to comply with Data Protection Laws.

### ANNEX 1 – DESCRIPTION OF PROCESSING

**Subject Matter and Duration of the Processing**
The subject matter of the Processing is the provision of the Offerings under the Agreement. The duration of the Processing is the Term of the Agreement and any additional period as described in section 9 of this DPA.

**Nature and Purpose of Processing**
Deslicer Processes Personal Data for the purposes of:

- providing, operating, maintaining and supporting the Offerings (including Deslicer AI, Deslicer DevOps and Deslicer Automation Platform);
- performing configuration management, policy enforcement, orchestration and CI/CD workflows as configured by Customer;
- generating analyses, insights, recommendations and automation Outputs based on Customer’s data;
- ensuring security, availability and performance of the Offerings; and
- improving and developing the Offerings, where permitted by the Agreement and this DPA.

**Types of Personal Data**
As described in section 2.2 of this DPA.

**Categories of Data Subjects**
As described in section 2.1 of this DPA.

**Frequency of Transfer**
Continuous for the duration of Customer’s use of the Offerings.

**Retention Period**
As described in section 9 of this DPA.

### ANNEX 2 – TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES

Deslicer implements a security program that includes, among other things, the following controls (as more fully described in the Security section of the General Terms and any applicable Security Exhibits):

- encryption of data in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols;
- access controls and authentication mechanisms, including role-based access control;
- network security measures, including segmentation and firewalls for hosted components;
- logging and monitoring of security-relevant events;
- vulnerability management, including patch management and security updates;
- incident response processes and procedures, including breach detection and notification;
- business continuity and disaster recovery planning;
- secure software development lifecycle practices, including code review and security testing; and
- regular security assessments and, where appropriate, penetration testing.

Customer acknowledges that these measures may be updated from time to time in accordance with section 4.3 of this DPA.

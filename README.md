## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I execute the DPA?
Contact legal@deslicer.com with subject "DPA Execution Request" and provide:
- Your legal entity name and address
- Company registration number
- Contact person details
- Whether you need data transfer addenda (EU SCCs, UK IDTA)

### How do I stay informed about changes?
- **Watch this repository** on GitHub
- **Subscribe to the commit feed:** https://github.com/deslicer/legal/commits/main.atom
- **Ensure your email is current** in your customer account

### Do these terms apply to all Deslicer services?
Yes, the General Terms apply to all Deslicer Offerings. Each Offering also has Specific Terms that provide additional service-specific provisions.

### Can I negotiate custom terms?
Enterprise customers may negotiate certain terms. Contact legal@deslicer.com to discuss custom requirements.

### Where can I find older versions?
Historical versions are available via Git commit history. Click "History" on any document to view previous versions.

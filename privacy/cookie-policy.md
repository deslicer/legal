## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember your preferences, and provide information to site owners about how the site is used.

---

## How We Use Cookies

We categorize cookies into the following types:

### Essential Cookies (Required)

These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function. They enable core features like security, authentication, and accessibility. You cannot disable these cookies as they are required for the service to operate.

| Cookie Name | Provider | Purpose | Retention |
|-------------|----------|---------|-----------|
| `sb-*-auth-token` | Supabase | Authentication session | Session |
| `cc_cookie` | CookieConsent | Stores your cookie preferences | 1 year |
| `__Host-csrf_token` | Deslicer | CSRF protection | Session |

### Analytics Cookies (Optional)

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use this data to improve our service.

| Cookie Name | Provider | Purpose | Retention |
|-------------|----------|---------|-----------|
| `ph_*` | PostHog | Page views, clicks, feature usage | 1 year |

**Provider Details:**
- **PostHog** (PostHog Inc.) - Analytics platform hosted in the EU (eu.posthog.com)
- IP addresses are truncated to anonymize user location
- No personally identifiable information is collected without consent
- Data is processed in accordance with GDPR requirements

### Session Replay & Monitoring Cookies (Optional)

These cookies allow us to record user interactions to diagnose errors, usability issues, and improve user experience. Sensitive fields are automatically masked.

| Cookie Name | Provider | Purpose | Retention |
|-------------|----------|---------|-----------|
| `sentry-*` | Sentry | Session replay, error diagnostics | Session |

**Provider Details:**
- **Sentry** (Functional Software, Inc.) - Error monitoring and session replay
- Form inputs are masked by default
- Sensitive data is automatically redacted
- Used only for debugging and UX improvement purposes

## Your Choices

### Managing Cookie Preferences

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time:

1. **On our website:** Click "Cookie Settings" in the footer or go to Account Settings > Cookie Preferences
2. **In your browser:** Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through settings

### Withdrawing Consent

You can withdraw your consent for optional cookies at any time. Simply:
1. Click "Cookie Settings" in the footer
2. Disable the categories you no longer wish to allow
3. Click "Save preferences"

Your preference will take effect immediately. Previously collected data may be retained for the retention periods specified above.

### Browser Settings

You can also control cookies through your browser settings:
- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-and-website-data-sfri11471/mac)
- [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

**Note:** Disabling essential cookies may prevent you from using certain features of our service.

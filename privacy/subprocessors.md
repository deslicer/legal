This document lists the subprocessors engaged by Deslicer AB ("Deslicer") to process Personal Data on behalf of Customers in connection with the Offerings, as referenced in the Deslicer Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and the Specific Terms for Deslicer Offerings.

Deslicer may engage third-party subprocessors to assist in providing the Offerings. This list is maintained in accordance with Article 28 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and section 4.4 of the Deslicer DPA.

## Changes to Subprocessors

In accordance with section 4.4 of the DPA, Deslicer will:

1. Provide advance notice of additions or replacements of subprocessors where required by applicable Data Protection Laws;
2. Allow Customers to object on reasonable grounds relating to data protection; and
3. Work with Customers to resolve objections, or permit termination of the affected Offering if objections cannot be resolved.

## Notification of Changes

Deslicer will notify Customers of material changes to this subprocessor list (additions or replacements) through the following methods:

**Standard Notification Method:**

All changes to this subprocessor list are published in this public repository at `github.com/deslicer/legal`. Customers can receive automatic notifications by:

1. **Watching this repository** on GitHub (recommended method):
   - Navigate to `github.com/deslicer/legal`
   - Click "Watch" → "All Activity"
   - GitHub will send email notifications for all updates to this repository
   - This provides immediate, automatic push notifications of any changes

2. **Subscribing to repository RSS feed** (alternative method):
   - Available at `github.com/deslicer/legal/commits/main.atom`

**Additional Notification for Enterprise Customers:**

For enterprise customers with specific notification requirements in their Order or where direct email notification has been agreed, Deslicer will also send direct email notifications to designated contacts.

**Consumer Subscription Email Notification (Optional):**

Individual consumers using Deslicer AI may opt in to receive subprocessor change notifications via their subscription email address. To enable this:

- Log in to your Deslicer account at [deslicer.ai](https://deslicer.ai)
- Navigate to your account settings and enable email notifications for subprocessor updates (typically found under notification preferences or privacy settings)

When enabled, we will send email notifications to your registered email address for material subprocessor changes, in addition to publishing changes in this repository.

**For Customers Who Cannot Use GitHub Notifications:**

Customers whose business or technical environment does not permit GitHub-based notifications (for example, due to firewall restrictions, corporate policies, or accessibility requirements) should contact [legal@deslicer.com](mailto:legal@deslicer.com) within 30 days of contract execution to arrange alternative notification methods. Alternative methods may include:

- Direct email to designated contacts (primary and backup)
- Notification via Customer's procurement portal or vendor management system
- Quarterly notification digest sent via secure email

Alternative notification arrangements will be documented in the Order or as an amendment to the Agreement.

Where alternative notification is in place, the standard GitHub notification timeline (at least 30 days advance notice) will be matched or exceeded via the agreed alternative channel. Alternative arrangements do not reduce Customer's objection rights under section 4.4 of the DPA.

**Timing:**

Where required by applicable Data Protection Laws (including GDPR Article 28), material subprocessor changes will be committed to this repository with at least 30 days advance notice before the new subprocessor begins processing Personal Data. Commit messages will clearly indicate:
- The effective date of the change
- The deadline for objections
- Contact information for questions or objections

**Customer Responsibility:**

Customers are responsible for:
- Setting up GitHub watch notifications or RSS feed subscriptions for this repository;
- Monitoring their chosen notification channel;
- Maintaining current email addresses for GitHub notifications;
- Promptly reviewing subprocessor change notifications;
- Raising objections within the specified time period if they have reasonable grounds relating to data protection.

**Alternative Arrangements:**

Customers who cannot use GitHub-based notifications for business or technical reasons should contact [legal@deslicer.com](mailto:legal@deslicer.com) to arrange alternative notification methods, which will be documented in their Order.

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

### 1.1 Purpose

This Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Policy sets out Deslicer AB's ("Deslicer" or "the Company") commitment to conducting business in a manner that is ethical, sustainable, and socially responsible. This Policy reflects our values and guides our relationships with employees, customers, partners, suppliers, and the communities in which we operate.

### 1.2 Scope

This Policy applies to:
- All Deslicer employees, officers, and directors
- Contractors and temporary workers engaged by Deslicer
- Deslicer's business operations and service delivery
- To the extent reasonably practicable, Deslicer's suppliers and business partners

### 1.3 Foundation

As a Swedish company, Deslicer operates within a legal framework that provides strong protections for human rights, labor standards, environmental responsibility, and ethical business conduct. This Policy builds upon those foundations and aligns with internationally recognized standards, including:
- United Nations Global Compact principles
- International Labour Organization (ILO) core conventions
- Universal Declaration of Human Rights
- OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises
- Swedish Environmental Code (Miljöbalken)

## 2. HUMAN RIGHTS AND LABOR STANDARDS

### 2.1 Respect for Human Rights

Deslicer is committed to respecting internationally recognized human rights in all our operations and business relationships. We:
- Support and respect the protection of internationally proclaimed human rights
- Ensure we are not complicit in human rights abuses
- Conduct due diligence to identify and address potential human rights impacts

### 2.2 Labor Rights and Working Conditions

Deslicer complies with Swedish labor law (Arbetsrättslagen and related legislation), which provides comprehensive protections that meet or exceed ILO core conventions. We are committed to:

**Freedom of Association and Collective Bargaining:**
- Employees have the right to form, join, or not join labor unions
- We respect the right to collective bargaining
- We maintain open dialogue with employee representatives

**Fair Employment:**
- All employment relationships are voluntary and based on mutual consent
- Employment terms are clearly documented and communicated
- Employees are free to terminate employment in accordance with applicable law and contract terms

**Working Hours and Rest:**
- We comply with Swedish Working Hours Act (Arbetstidslagen)
- Standard working hours do not exceed legal maximums
- Employees receive appropriate rest periods and annual leave

**Fair Compensation:**
- All employees receive at least the applicable minimum wage or collectively agreed wage rates
- Compensation is competitive and reflects skills, experience, and market conditions
- Wages are paid regularly and in accordance with employment agreements

**Health and Safety:**
- We provide a safe and healthy working environment
- We comply with Swedish Work Environment Act (Arbetsmiljölagen)
- We conduct risk assessments and implement appropriate preventive measures
- We provide necessary training and equipment for safe work
- Employees have the right to report unsafe conditions without retaliation

---

## 3. PROHIBITION OF FORCED LABOR AND CHILD LABOR

### 3.1 No Forced Labor

Deslicer absolutely prohibits all forms of forced, bonded, indentured, or involuntary labor. We:
- Do not require employees to surrender identity documents, pay deposits, or provide financial guarantees as a condition of employment
- Allow employees to terminate employment in accordance with applicable law and reasonable notice periods
- Do not use or support any form of modern slavery or human trafficking

### 3.2 No Child Labor

Deslicer does not employ children. Consistent with Swedish law and ILO Convention 138:
- Minimum age for employment is 18 years for all positions
- We do not engage in or support the use of child labor in our supply chain
- We support education and protection of children's rights

---

## 4. NON-DISCRIMINATION, DIVERSITY, AND INCLUSION

### 4.1 Equal Opportunity

Deslicer is committed to providing equal opportunity in all aspects of employment. We comply with Swedish Discrimination Act (Diskrimineringslagen) and prohibit discrimination based on:
- Gender, gender identity, or gender expression
- Ethnicity, national origin, or citizenship
- Religion or other belief
- Disability
- Sexual orientation
- Age
- Other characteristics protected by applicable law

### 4.2 Diversity and Inclusion

We recognize that diversity strengthens our organization. We are committed to:
- Creating an inclusive workplace culture where all individuals are valued and respected
- Promoting diversity in recruitment, retention, and advancement
- Providing reasonable accommodations for individuals with disabilities
- Fostering an environment free from harassment, bullying, and discrimination

### 4.3 Harassment-Free Workplace

Deslicer maintains zero tolerance for harassment, including sexual harassment, bullying, or any form of abusive conduct. All employees have the right to:
- Work in an environment free from harassment and intimidation
- Report concerns without fear of retaliation
- Receive prompt and appropriate response to complaints

## 5. ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY

### 5.1 Environmental Commitment

Deslicer is committed to minimizing the environmental impact of our operations and services. Our environmental commitments are detailed in our separate Environmental and Sustainability Policy and include:
- Compliance with applicable environmental laws and regulations
- Energy efficiency and preference for renewable energy
- Waste minimization and responsible disposal
- Greenhouse gas emissions measurement and reduction
- Sustainable procurement practices

### 5.2 Environmental Management

We strive to:
- Integrate environmental considerations into business decisions
- Set environmental objectives and monitor progress
- Promote environmental awareness among employees and partners
- Continuously improve our environmental performance

---

## 6. ETHICAL BUSINESS CONDUCT

### 6.1 Anti-Corruption and Anti-Bribery

Deslicer maintains a zero-tolerance approach to corruption and bribery. Our commitments are detailed in our separate Anti-Corruption and Ethics Policy and include:
- Compliance with all applicable anti-corruption laws, including Swedish Penal Code, UK Bribery Act, US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act, and French Sapin 2 Law
- Prohibition of bribes, kickbacks, and improper payments
- Appropriate due diligence on business partners and intermediaries
- Accurate books and records

### 6.2 Fair Competition

We compete fairly and in accordance with applicable competition and antitrust laws. We:
- Do not engage in anti-competitive practices, price fixing, or market manipulation
- Respect intellectual property rights of others
- Compete on the merits of our products and services

### 6.3 Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict or appear to conflict with Deslicer's interests. Employees must:
- Disclose potential conflicts of interest to management
- Refrain from using company position for personal gain
- Maintain objectivity in business decisions

### 6.4 Data Protection and Privacy

We respect the privacy rights of individuals and comply with applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We:
- Process personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Respect individuals' rights regarding their personal data
- Maintain comprehensive Data Processing Agreement and privacy practices

---

## 7. SUPPLY CHAIN RESPONSIBILITY

### 7.1 Supplier Standards

Deslicer expects our suppliers and business partners to adhere to standards consistent with this CSR Policy. We:
- Maintain a Supplier Code of Conduct setting forth our expectations
- Conduct appropriate due diligence on suppliers
- Encourage suppliers to adopt similar CSR commitments
- Reserve the right to terminate relationships with suppliers who persistently violate CSR principles

### 7.2 Subprocessor Management

For suppliers who process personal data on behalf of Deslicer (subprocessors), we:
- Require written agreements with appropriate data protection obligations
- Conduct due diligence on data protection and security practices
- Monitor compliance with data protection requirements
- Maintain a list of subprocessors available to customers

## 8. COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

### 8.1 Corporate Citizenship

Deslicer strives to be a responsible corporate citizen. As a growing technology company, we:
- Support professional development and knowledge sharing in our technology communities
- Consider environmental and social impacts in our business decisions
- Encourage employee participation in community activities where appropriate
- Grow our community engagement programs as our business matures

### 8.2 Stakeholder Engagement

We maintain open and transparent communication with stakeholders, including:
- Employees and their representatives
- Customers and partners
- Regulators and government authorities
- Civil society organizations where relevant

## 9. REPORTING AND WHISTLEBLOWING

### 9.1 Speak-Up Culture

Deslicer encourages employees and stakeholders to report concerns about potential violations of this Policy, applicable laws, or ethical standards. We are committed to:
- Providing channels for reporting concerns
- Investigating reports promptly and thoroughly
- Protecting individuals who report concerns in good faith from retaliation
- Taking appropriate corrective action when violations are confirmed

### 9.2 Reporting Channels

Concerns may be reported through:
- Direct supervisor or management
- Chief Executive Officer
- External whistleblower hotline (to be established by Q2 2026)
- Email to: [compliance@deslicer.com]

### 9.3 Non-Retaliation

Deslicer prohibits retaliation against any individual who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation. Retaliation is itself a violation of this Policy and will result in disciplinary action.

## 10. GOVERNANCE AND IMPLEMENTATION

### 10.1 Responsibility

**Overall Accountability:** Chief Executive Officer
**Day-to-Day Implementation:** All managers and supervisors
**Policy Review:** Chief Executive Officer with input from legal counsel

### 10.2 Training and Awareness

Deslicer provides appropriate training and communication to ensure employees understand:
- The requirements of this CSR Policy
- Their individual responsibilities
- How to report concerns
- Available resources and support

Training is provided:
- During onboarding for new employees
- Annually for all employees (refresher training)
- On an ad-hoc basis when policy updates occur

### 10.3 Monitoring and Reporting

Deslicer monitors compliance with this Policy through:
- Regular management review and assessment
- Employee feedback and surveys
- Incident tracking and investigation
- External audits where appropriate

We are committed to transparency and will:
- Provide CSR-related information to customers and partners upon reasonable request
- Respond to stakeholder inquiries about our CSR practices
- Publish annual or biennial CSR statements as our reporting capabilities mature
- Cooperate with customer CSR audits and assessments

### 10.4 Continuous Improvement

We recognize that CSR is an ongoing journey. We commit to:
- Regularly reviewing and updating this Policy (at least every two years)
- Setting measurable CSR objectives and monitoring progress
- Learning from incidents and implementing improvements
- Staying informed about evolving CSR standards and best practices
- Scaling our CSR programs as our company grows

### 10.5 Consequences of Non-Compliance

Violations of this Policy may result in:
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment
- Termination of business relationships with partners or suppliers
- Legal action where appropriate
- Remediation plans and corrective measures

- Participating in stakeholder consultation processes where appropriate

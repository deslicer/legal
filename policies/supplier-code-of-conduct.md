## 1. INTRODUCTION

### 1.1 Purpose

This Supplier Code of Conduct ("Code") sets forth Deslicer AB's ("Deslicer" or "we") expectations for our suppliers, vendors, service providers, subprocessors, and other business partners (collectively, "Suppliers"). Deslicer is committed to conducting business ethically, sustainably, and in compliance with applicable laws, and we expect our Suppliers to uphold similar standards.

### 1.2 Scope

This Code applies to all Suppliers who provide goods or services to Deslicer, including but not limited to:
- Cloud infrastructure and technology service providers
- Software and SaaS vendors
- Professional services firms (legal, accounting, consulting)
- Data subprocessors
- Office suppliers and equipment vendors
- Marketing and communications agencies
- Contractors and temporary staffing providers

### 1.3 Supplier Responsibility

By doing business with Deslicer, Suppliers agree to:
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations
- Adhere to the standards set forth in this Code
- Communicate these standards to their own employees, contractors, and sub-suppliers
- Cooperate with Deslicer's reasonable monitoring and verification efforts
- Promptly notify Deslicer of any actual or potential violations

Deslicer reserves the right to terminate business relationships with Suppliers who fail to meet these standards or fail to implement timely corrective actions.

## 2. LEGAL COMPLIANCE

### 2.1 General Compliance

Suppliers must comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards in all jurisdictions where they operate, including but not limited to laws relating to:
- Labor and employment
- Health and safety
- Environmental protection
- Anti-corruption and anti-bribery
- Data protection and privacy
- Export controls and economic sanctions
- Tax and financial reporting
- Competition and antitrust
- Intellectual property

### 2.2 Permits and Licenses

Suppliers must obtain and maintain all necessary permits, licenses, registrations, and approvals required for their business operations and provision of goods or services to Deslicer.

## 3. HUMAN RIGHTS AND LABOR STANDARDS

### 3.1 Respect for Human Rights

Suppliers must respect internationally recognized human rights as outlined in:
- Universal Declaration of Human Rights
- International Labour Organization (ILO) Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work
- UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights

Suppliers must not be complicit in human rights abuses and must conduct appropriate due diligence to identify and address human rights impacts in their operations and supply chains.

### 3.2 Prohibition of Forced Labor

Suppliers must not use any form of forced, bonded, indentured, involuntary, or trafficked labor. Specifically, Suppliers must:
- Ensure all work is voluntary
- Not require workers to surrender identity documents, pay deposits, or provide financial guarantees as condition of employment
- Allow workers to terminate employment with reasonable notice
- Not impose unreasonable restrictions on workers' freedom of movement
- Not use physical or psychological coercion

### 3.3 Prohibition of Child Labor

Suppliers must not employ children. Specifically:
- Minimum age for employment must be 15 years (or 14 if permitted by local law under ILO Convention 138)
- Minimum age should be 18 for hazardous work
- Suppliers must comply with all applicable child labor laws and ILO standards
- If child labor is discovered, Supplier must implement appropriate remediation measures

### 3.4 Freedom of Association and Collective Bargaining

Suppliers must respect workers' rights to:
- Form, join, or not join labor unions or worker organizations
- Engage in collective bargaining
- Peaceful assembly and freedom of association

Where these rights are restricted by law, Suppliers should facilitate alternative means of independent worker representation and dialogue.

### 3.5 Non-Discrimination and Equal Opportunity

Suppliers must provide equal employment opportunities and treat workers with dignity and respect. Suppliers must not discriminate based on:
- Race, color, ethnicity, or national origin
- Gender, gender identity, or gender expression
- Sexual orientation
- Religion or belief
- Age
- Disability or medical condition
- Marital or family status
- Pregnancy
- Political affiliation
- Other characteristics protected by applicable law

Suppliers must ensure fair recruitment, compensation, promotion, training, and termination practices.

### 3.6 Fair Wages and Benefits

Suppliers must:
- Pay wages that meet or exceed legal minimum wages or appropriate prevailing industry wages, whichever is higher
- Comply with all legal requirements regarding wages, overtime, and benefits
- Provide clear written information about wages, deductions, and terms of employment
- Ensure wages are sufficient to meet basic needs and provide discretionary income
- Not use wage deductions as disciplinary measure except as permitted by law

### 3.7 Working Hours

Suppliers must comply with applicable laws regarding working hours, breaks, and rest periods. As general guidance:
- Standard working hours should not exceed 48 hours per week
- Overtime should not exceed 12 hours per week
- Workers should receive at least one day off per seven-day period
- Overtime should be voluntary and compensated at premium rates as required by law

### 3.8 Humane Treatment

Suppliers must treat all workers with dignity and respect. Suppliers must not engage in or tolerate:
- Physical abuse or corporal punishment
- Sexual or other harassment
- Verbal abuse or threats
- Mental or psychological coercion
- Bullying or intimidation
- Unreasonable restrictions on movement within workplace

## 4. HEALTH AND SAFETY

### 4.1 Safe Working Environment

Suppliers must provide a safe and healthy working environment that prevents accidents and injury to health. Suppliers must:
- Comply with all applicable health and safety laws and regulations
- Implement appropriate health and safety management systems
- Identify and assess workplace hazards and risks
- Implement preventive and protective measures
- Provide appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) at no cost to workers
- Ensure machinery and equipment are properly maintained and safeguarded

### 4.2 Emergency Preparedness

Suppliers must:
- Identify potential emergency situations and events
- Develop and implement emergency response procedures
- Provide clear emergency exits and evacuation routes
- Conduct emergency drills and training
- Maintain appropriate firefighting and first aid equipment
- Post emergency contact information visibly

### 4.3 Occupational Health

Suppliers must:
- Control worker exposure to hazardous substances (chemical, biological, physical)
- Provide appropriate ventilation, lighting, and temperature control
- Ensure access to clean toilet facilities, potable water, and sanitary food storage
- Provide appropriate medical monitoring where required
- Implement measures to prevent work-related injuries and illnesses

### 4.4 Health and Safety Training

Suppliers must provide workers with appropriate health and safety training, including:
- Initial training for new workers
- Job-specific safety training
- Refresher training as needed
- Training in workers' native language or language they understand

### 4.5 Management Systems

Suppliers are encouraged to implement health and safety management systems aligned with recognized standards such as ISO 45001 or equivalent.

## 5. ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY

### 5.1 Environmental Compliance

Suppliers must comply with all applicable environmental laws, regulations, and permit requirements, including those relating to:
- Air emissions
- Water discharge
- Waste management
- Hazardous substances
- Resource conservation
- Climate change and greenhouse gas emissions

### 5.2 Environmental Management

Suppliers must implement appropriate environmental management practices, including:
- Identifying environmental aspects and impacts of their operations
- Setting environmental objectives and targets
- Monitoring and measuring environmental performance
- Implementing pollution prevention and waste reduction measures
- Continuously improving environmental performance

Suppliers are encouraged to implement environmental management systems aligned with ISO 14001 or equivalent standards.

### 5.3 Climate Change and Greenhouse Gas Emissions

Suppliers are encouraged to:
- Measure and disclose greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1, 2, and relevant Scope 3)
- Set emissions reduction targets aligned with climate science
- Work towards carbon neutrality or net-zero emissions
- Support renewable energy use and energy efficiency
- Consider alignment with Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) or similar frameworks

Deslicer may request environmental performance data from Suppliers to support Deslicer's own environmental reporting obligations.

### 5.4 Energy and Resource Efficiency

Suppliers should:
- Minimize energy consumption and improve energy efficiency
- Reduce water consumption and protect water resources
- Minimize waste generation and maximize reuse and recycling
- Use sustainable materials and resources where feasible

### 5.5 Hazardous Substances

Suppliers must:
- Properly manage, store, and dispose of hazardous materials and waste
- Comply with regulations such as REACH, RoHS, and similar substance restrictions
- Provide Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for hazardous materials as required
- Train workers on safe handling of hazardous substances

### 5.6 Biodiversity and Ecosystem Protection

Suppliers should:
- Avoid operations that harm critical ecosystems or protected areas
- Support biodiversity conservation where relevant to operations
- Use materials from sustainable and responsibly managed sources (e.g., FSC-certified paper)

## 6. ANTI-CORRUPTION AND BUSINESS ETHICS

### 6.1 Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption

Suppliers must maintain the highest standards of integrity and comply with all applicable anti-corruption and anti-bribery laws, including:
- UK Bribery Act 2010
- US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA)
- French Sapin 2 Law
- Swedish Penal Code
- OECD Anti-Bribery Convention

Suppliers must not, directly or indirectly:
- Offer, promise, give, request, or accept bribes or improper payments
- Make facilitation payments or "grease payments"
- Provide anything of value to government officials or private parties to improperly influence decisions
- Engage in commercial bribery, kickbacks, or corrupt practices
- Launder money or engage in fraud

### 6.2 Gifts and Hospitality

Suppliers may provide modest business courtesies (gifts, meals, entertainment) to Deslicer personnel only if such courtesies are:
- Reasonable and proportionate in value
- Transparent and properly recorded
- Appropriate to the business context
- Not intended to improperly influence decisions
- In compliance with Deslicer's Anti-Corruption and Ethics Policy

Suppliers must never offer cash, cash equivalents, or lavish gifts or entertainment to Deslicer personnel.

### 6.3 Conflicts of Interest

Suppliers must:
- Disclose any actual or potential conflicts of interest in their relationship with Deslicer
- Avoid situations where personal interests could improperly influence business decisions
- Not exploit their business relationship with Deslicer for personal gain
- Ensure objective and impartial advice and services

### 6.4 Fair Competition

Suppliers must:
- Compete fairly and in accordance with all applicable competition and antitrust laws
- Not engage in price fixing, bid rigging, market allocation, or other anti-competitive practices
- Respect competitors and market integrity

### 6.5 Confidential Information and Intellectual Property

Suppliers must:
- Protect Deslicer's confidential and proprietary information
- Use confidential information only for the authorized purpose
- Respect intellectual property rights of Deslicer and third parties
- Not infringe patents, trademarks, copyrights, or trade secrets
- Comply with all licensing terms for software and technology

### 6.6 Accurate Records and Transparency

Suppliers must:
- Maintain accurate books, records, and accounts
- Provide truthful and accurate information to Deslicer
- Not falsify records or make misleading representations
- Comply with all applicable financial reporting and tax laws
- Issue accurate invoices that reflect actual goods or services provided

### 6.7 Insider Trading

Suppliers must not use material non-public information obtained through relationship with Deslicer for trading securities or providing tips to others.

## 7. DATA PROTECTION AND INFORMATION SECURITY

### 7.1 Data Protection and Privacy

Suppliers who process personal data on behalf of Deslicer or have access to personal data through their relationship with Deslicer must:
- Comply with all applicable data protection laws, including EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- Process personal data only in accordance with Deslicer's instructions and applicable Data Processing Agreements
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Ensure confidentiality of personal data
- Notify Deslicer of any personal data breaches without undue delay
- Assist Deslicer with data subject rights requests and compliance obligations
- Delete or return personal data upon termination or as instructed

### 7.2 Information Security

Suppliers must:
- Implement appropriate security controls to protect Deslicer's information and systems
- Maintain confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information
- Protect against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction
- Implement access controls, encryption, and secure transmission protocols
- Conduct security risk assessments and implement appropriate safeguards
- Notify Deslicer promptly of any security incidents affecting Deslicer's data or systems

### 7.3 Cybersecurity

Suppliers should:
- Implement cybersecurity best practices aligned with recognized frameworks (e.g., NIST, ISO 27001)
- Maintain security patches and updates
- Conduct regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing
- Implement incident response and business continuity plans
- Provide security awareness training to personnel

### 7.4 Subprocessing

Suppliers who engage subprocessors to process Deslicer data must:
- Obtain Deslicer's prior written authorization for subprocessors where required
- Ensure subprocessors are bound by equivalent data protection and security obligations
- Remain fully liable for subprocessor performance

## 8. INTERNATIONAL TRADE AND SANCTIONS COMPLIANCE

### 8.1 Export Controls

Suppliers must comply with all applicable export control laws and regulations, including:
- Swedish export control regulations
- EU Dual-Use Regulation
- US Export Administration Regulations (where applicable)

Suppliers must obtain necessary licenses and authorizations before exporting controlled items or technology.

### 8.2 Economic Sanctions

Suppliers must comply with international economic sanctions and embargoes imposed by:
- United Nations
- European Union
- Sweden
- Other applicable jurisdictions

Suppliers must:
- Screen business partners and transactions against sanctions lists
- Not engage with sanctioned individuals, entities, or countries
- Not facilitate transactions that violate sanctions
- Represent that neither Supplier nor its beneficial owners are subject to applicable sanctions

### 8.3 Customs and Import Compliance

Suppliers must:
- Accurately classify goods for customs purposes
- Provide truthful and complete information to customs authorities
- Pay all applicable duties, taxes, and fees
- Comply with country-of-origin and labeling requirements

## 9. RESPONSIBLE SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT

### 9.1 Extension to Sub-Suppliers

Suppliers must:
- Communicate the principles of this Code to their own suppliers and subcontractors
- Conduct appropriate due diligence on sub-suppliers
- Monitor sub-supplier compliance with equivalent standards
- Take corrective action if sub-suppliers fail to meet standards

### 9.2 Conflict Minerals

If applicable, Suppliers must:
- Exercise due diligence on source and chain of custody of minerals (tin, tantalum, tungsten, gold)
- Work towards ensuring minerals do not finance armed conflict or human rights abuses
- Respond to Deslicer's reasonable requests for conflict minerals information
- Support supply chain transparency

### 9.3 Responsible Sourcing

Suppliers are encouraged to:
- Source materials and services responsibly and sustainably
- Prefer suppliers with strong ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) performance
- Support fair trade and ethical sourcing practices
- Consider lifecycle environmental and social impacts of products and services

## 10. MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

### 10.1 Management Commitment

Suppliers should:
- Demonstrate leadership commitment to the principles of this Code
- Allocate appropriate resources for compliance and improvement
- Assign clear responsibility for compliance
- Integrate these standards into business strategy and operations

### 10.2 Policies and Procedures

Suppliers are encouraged to:
- Adopt written policies addressing the topics in this Code
- Implement procedures and controls to ensure compliance
- Regularly review and update policies based on changing risks and requirements

### 10.3 Training and Communication

Suppliers should:
- Provide appropriate training to personnel on their responsibilities
- Communicate expectations clearly throughout the organization
- Ensure workers understand their rights and how to report concerns

### 10.4 Monitoring and Assessment

Suppliers should:
- Monitor compliance with this Code and applicable laws
- Conduct regular risk assessments
- Implement corrective and preventive actions
- Track performance through appropriate metrics and KPIs

### 10.5 Continuous Improvement

Suppliers are expected to:
- Continuously improve performance on the standards in this Code
- Set measurable objectives and targets
- Learn from incidents and near-misses
- Adopt industry best practices
- Innovate to enhance sustainability and responsibility

## 11. REPORTING AND WHISTLEBLOWING

### 11.1 Internal Reporting Mechanisms

Suppliers should establish and maintain confidential reporting channels allowing workers and stakeholders to report concerns about violations of this Code, applicable laws, or ethical standards without fear of retaliation.

### 11.2 Reporting to Deslicer

Suppliers must:
- Promptly notify Deslicer of any actual or suspected violations of this Code
- Cooperate with Deslicer's inquiries and investigations
- Provide timely and accurate information about incidents or concerns

Reports to Deslicer may be made to: compliance@deslicer.com

### 11.3 Non-Retaliation

Suppliers must not retaliate against anyone who reports concerns in good faith or participates in investigations. Retaliation is a serious violation of this Code.

---

## 12. DESLICER'S RIGHTS AND SUPPLIER OBLIGATIONS

### 12.1 Verification and Audits

Deslicer reserves the right to:
- Request information and documentation regarding Supplier's compliance with this Code
- Conduct or commission audits or assessments of Supplier facilities and practices (subject to reasonable notice and confidentiality)
- Engage third-party auditors or assessors
- Include audit rights in supplier agreements

Suppliers must:
- Cooperate with reasonable verification efforts
- Provide requested information and documentation within reasonable timeframes
- Allow access for audits as agreed in contracts (typically remote audits)
- Address findings and implement corrective actions

### 12.2 Transparency and Reporting

Suppliers must:
- Respond to Deslicer's reasonable requests for information related to this Code
- Complete supplier questionnaires and self-assessments honestly and accurately
- Provide certifications or attestations as requested
- Disclose any material changes in ownership, operations, or risk profile

### 12.3 Corrective Action

If Deslicer identifies non-compliance with this Code, Supplier must:
- Acknowledge the issue promptly
- Develop a corrective action plan within timeframe specified by Deslicer
- Implement corrective actions effectively
- Provide progress updates as requested
- Prevent recurrence

### 12.4 Consequences of Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with this Code may result in:
- Requirement for corrective action plan
- Enhanced monitoring and auditing
- Termination of business relationship
- Removal from approved supplier list
- Legal action where appropriate

Deslicer reserves the right to terminate agreements immediately for serious violations, including:
- Use of forced or child labor
- Serious health and safety violations resulting in worker harm
- Bribery, corruption, or fraud
- Material misrepresentation or falsification of records
- Criminal violations
- Refusal to cooperate with reasonable compliance efforts

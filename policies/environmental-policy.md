## 1. PURPOSE AND COMMITMENT

### 1.1 Purpose

This Environmental and Sustainability Policy establishes Deslicer AB's ("Deslicer" or "the Company") commitment to environmental responsibility and sustainable business practices. As a provider of software solutions for log management and observability optimization, we recognize our responsibility to minimize environmental impact and contribute to global sustainability goals.

### 1.2 Core Commitment

Deslicer is committed to:
- Conducting business in an environmentally responsible manner
- Complying with all applicable environmental laws and regulations
- Minimizing the environmental footprint of our operations and services
- Promoting energy efficiency and renewable energy use
- Measuring, reporting, and reducing our greenhouse gas emissions
- Continuously improving our environmental performance
- Integrating sustainability considerations into business decisions

### 1.3 Scope

This Policy applies to:
- Deslicer's corporate operations and offices
- Development and delivery of software products and services
- Infrastructure and technology choices
- Supply chain and procurement decisions
- Employee business travel and remote work practices

## 3. CLIMATE CHANGE AND GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS

### 3.1 Climate Commitment

Deslicer recognizes the urgency of climate change and commits to:
- Measuring and understanding our greenhouse gas emissions footprint
- Setting reduction targets aligned with climate science
- Working toward carbon neutrality in our operations
- Supporting our customers' sustainability goals

### 3.2 GHG Emissions Measurement and Reporting

**Phase 1: Baseline Establishment (Months 1-12)**

Within 12 months of this Policy's effective date, Deslicer will:
- Establish organizational-level greenhouse gas emissions baseline
- Measure emissions across applicable scopes:
  - **Scope 1:** Direct emissions from owned or controlled sources (if any)
  - **Scope 2:** Indirect emissions from purchased electricity, heating, and cooling
  - **Scope 3:** Other indirect emissions in our value chain (business travel, cloud infrastructure, employee commuting, purchased goods and services)
- Implement carbon accounting methodology aligned with:
  - GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard, or
  - ISO 14064-1 (Greenhouse gases — Specification with guidance for quantification and reporting)
- Select and implement carbon accounting software or service (e.g., Watershed, Normative, Minimum, Planetly)

**Phase 2: Annual Reporting (Beginning Month 24)**

Beginning 24 months from this Policy's effective date, Deslicer will:
- Publish annual organizational-level GHG emissions report
- Report emissions by scope (Scope 1, 2, and relevant Scope 3 categories)
- Disclose calculation methodologies and data sources
- Track emissions trends year-over-year
- Provide emissions data to customers upon reasonable request for their Scope 3 reporting

**Phase 3: Reduction Targets (Within 24 months)**

Within 24 months of this Policy's effective date, Deslicer will:
- Establish greenhouse gas emissions reduction targets
- Consider alignment with science-based targets (e.g., Science Based Targets initiative - SBTi)
- Evaluate pathways to carbon neutrality or net zero emissions
- Develop implementation roadmap for emissions reductions

### 3.3 Emissions Allocation for On-Premises Software

For Deslicer software deployed on customer premises:
- **Operational emissions** (energy consumption, cooling, hardware) are the **customer's responsibility** and fall within customer's Scope 2 and 3 emissions
- Deslicer will provide general guidance on energy-efficient deployment practices where feasible
- Customers are responsible for measuring and reporting operational emissions associated with on-premises deployments

For Deslicer SaaS services:
- Deslicer is responsible for measuring emissions associated with cloud infrastructure and service delivery
- Deslicer will work toward providing customers with emissions data associated with their service usage for customer Scope 3 reporting
- Allocation methodologies will be developed as measurement capabilities mature

## 4. ENERGY MANAGEMENT

### 4.1 Energy Efficiency

Deslicer commits to minimizing energy consumption through:
- Selecting energy-efficient office equipment and appliances
- Implementing power management settings on computers and devices
- Encouraging remote work to reduce commuting and office energy use
- Optimizing software code and architecture for computational efficiency
- Choosing cloud providers and data centers with high energy efficiency (Power Usage Effectiveness - PUE)

### 4.2 Renewable Energy

Deslicer expresses preference for renewable energy sources:
- **Office operations:** Select office spaces with renewable energy supply where available and commercially reasonable
- **Cloud infrastructure:** Prioritize cloud service providers committed to 100% renewable energy
  - Evaluate providers based on renewable energy commitments and transparency
  - Consider providers' carbon neutrality goals and achievements
  - Monitor progress toward renewable energy goals in provider selection and contract renewals

### 4.3 Cloud Provider Environmental Criteria

When selecting cloud infrastructure providers, Deslicer considers:
- Renewable energy commitments and achievements
- Carbon neutrality or net-zero targets and progress
- Energy efficiency (PUE) of data centers
- Transparency in environmental reporting
- Availability of carbon footprint data and tools
- Commitment to circular economy and hardware lifecycle management

Current major cloud providers' renewable energy status (as of 2025):
- AWS: Committed to 100% renewable energy and net-zero carbon by 2040
- Microsoft Azure: Carbon negative by 2030, 100% renewable energy by 2025
- Google Cloud: Carbon neutral since 2007, 100% renewable energy matched annually

## 5. WASTE MANAGEMENT

### 5.1 Waste Minimization

Deslicer commits to minimizing waste generation through:
- Paperless operations where feasible (digital documentation, electronic signatures)
- Reusable and durable office supplies and equipment
- Reduction of single-use items in office operations
- Right-sizing equipment purchases to avoid obsolescence

### 5.2 Waste Segregation and Recycling

We implement appropriate waste segregation and recycling:
- Separate collection of recyclable materials (paper, cardboard, plastic, metal, glass)
- Compliance with local waste management regulations
- Use of certified recycling and waste disposal services

### 5.3 Electronic Waste (E-Waste)

For electronic equipment at end of life:
- Use certified e-waste recycling services that comply with WEEE Directive and Swedish regulations
- Data destruction in accordance with data protection requirements before disposal
- Preference for equipment refurbishment and reuse where secure and practical
- Extend equipment lifecycle through maintenance and upgrades where feasible

## 7. BUSINESS TRAVEL AND TRANSPORTATION

### 7.1 Travel Reduction

Deslicer minimizes business travel environmental impact through:
- Remote work and distributed team model as primary operating mode
- Video conferencing for meetings where feasible
- Bundling trips to maximize efficiency when travel is necessary
- Encouraging regional in-person meetings to minimize long-distance travel

### 7.2 Sustainable Travel Options

When business travel is necessary:
- Prefer train over air travel for distances under 500 km where practical
- Select direct flights over connections to reduce emissions
- Consider carbon offset programs for unavoidable air travel
- Use public transportation, shared rides, or cycling/walking for local travel
- Select hotels and accommodations with environmental certifications where available

### 7.3 Company Vehicles

If company vehicles are acquired in the future:
- Prioritize electric or hybrid vehicles
- Implement fuel efficiency and maintenance programs
- Consider car sharing or fleet management services

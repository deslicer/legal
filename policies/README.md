## Overview

This directory contains Deslicer's public corporate policies governing corporate social responsibility, environmental sustainability, and supplier management. These policies demonstrate our commitment to ethical business practices, environmental stewardship, and responsible supply chain management.

---

## Available Policies

### [Corporate Social Responsibility Policy](csr-policy.md)
**Effective Date:** January 7, 2026
**Next Review:** January 2028

Our CSR Policy establishes Deslicer's commitment to:
- Respecting internationally recognized human rights
- Complying with labor standards and ILO core conventions
- Providing equal opportunity and non-discrimination
- Maintaining safe and healthy working conditions
- Ethical business conduct and anti-corruption
- Data protection and privacy

This policy is aligned with:
- UN Global Compact principles
- International Labour Organization (ILO) core conventions
- Universal Declaration of Human Rights
- OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises
- UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights

---

### [Environmental and Sustainability Policy](environmental-policy.md)
**Effective Date:** January 7, 2026
**Next Review:** January 2027

Our Environmental Policy establishes Deslicer's commitment to:
- Compliance with environmental laws and regulations
- Minimizing environmental impact of operations and services
- Measuring and reducing greenhouse gas emissions
- Energy efficiency and renewable energy preference
- Waste minimization and responsible disposal
- Continuous environmental improvement

**GHG Emissions Roadmap:**
- Baseline establishment within 12 months (January 2027)
- Annual reporting beginning 24 months (January 2028)
- Emissions reduction targets within 24 months
- Alignment with GHG Protocol and consideration of Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi)

---

### [Supplier Code of Conduct](supplier-code-of-conduct.md)
**Effective Date:** January 7, 2026
**Next Review:** January 2028

Our Supplier Code establishes expectations for suppliers, vendors, and business partners regarding:
- Legal compliance (anti-corruption, sanctions, competition law)
- Human rights and labor standards
- Health and safety
- Environmental responsibility
- Business ethics and data protection
- Audit rights and reporting mechanisms

This Code demonstrates that Deslicer applies similar CSR and ethical standards throughout our supply chain.

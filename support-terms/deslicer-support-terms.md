# Deslicer Support Terms

<img src="../media/logo.png" alt="Deslicer logo" width="300" />

Last Updated: January 7, 2026

These Support Terms govern the support and maintenance services provided by Deslicer AB ("Deslicer") for Deslicer Offerings, as referenced in the Deslicer General Terms.

## 1. Scope of Support

### 1.1 Applicability

These Support Terms apply to all Offerings unless otherwise specified in the Order. The specific Support Program included with an Offering will be identified in the Order.

**Enterprise vs. Consumer Support:** The support programs described in section 2 (Standard, Premium, and Enterprise Support) apply to enterprise customers governed by the Deslicer General Terms. Individual consumers governed by the Deslicer Consumer Terms receive support through the consumer support program described in section 2.4.

### 1.2 Support Services

Support services include:

- Technical assistance for installation, configuration, and use of Offerings;
- Troubleshooting and diagnosis of issues;
- Provision of Updates and Enhancements for On-Premises Software (where applicable);
- Access to Documentation and knowledge base resources;
- Bug fixes and patches for material defects.

## 2. Support Programs

Deslicer offers the following standard Support Programs:

### 2.1 Standard Support

**Coverage Hours:** Business hours (10:00-16:00 CET/CEST), Monday through Friday, excluding Swedish public holidays.

**Response Targets:**
- **Severity 1 (Critical):** Initial response within 8 business hours
- **Severity 2 (High):** Initial response within 16 business hours
- **Severity 3 (Medium):** Initial response within 3 business days
- **Severity 4 (Low):** Initial response within 5 business days

**Channels:** Email.

**Included with:** All paid subscriptions unless otherwise specified.

### 2.2 Premium Support

**Coverage Hours:** Extended hours (08:00-17:00 CET/CEST), Monday through Friday, excluding Swedish public holidays.

**Response Targets:**
- **Severity 1 (Critical):** Initial response within 4 business hours
- **Severity 2 (High):** Initial response within 8 business hours
- **Severity 3 (Medium):** Initial response within 2 business day
- **Severity 4 (Low):** Initial response within 3 business days

**Channels:** Email, phone, and dedicated support contact.

**Additional Benefits:**
- Priority handling of support requests;
- Named Technical Account Manager;
- Access to senior support engineers;
- Quarterly review calls (for annual subscriptions).

**Availability:** Available as an add-on to any paid subscription.

### 2.3 Enterprise Support

**Coverage Hours:** 24/7/365 (24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including Swedish public holidays).

**Response Targets:**
- **Severity 1 (Critical):** Initial response within 1 hour (any time)
- **Severity 2 (High):** Initial response within 2 hours (business hours) / 4 hours (non-business hours)
- **Severity 3 (Medium):** Initial response within 4 business hours
- **Severity 4 (Low):** Initial response within 2 business days

**Channels:** Email, web portal, phone, dedicated support contact, and emergency hotline.

**Additional Benefits:**
- Named Technical Account Manager;
- Proactive monitoring and guidance;
- Quarterly business reviews;
- Priority access to product roadmap discussions;
- Expedited handling of Enhancement requests.

**Availability:** Enterprise Support is not included by default with any Offering. It is available only under a separate Enterprise Support Agreement, which sets out specific commercial terms and may include the use of external or third-party help desks and support providers engaged by Deslicer to deliver the Enterprise Support services.

Where external or third-party support providers process Personal Data on Deslicer's behalf, such providers are engaged as subprocessors in accordance with the Data Processing Agreement and are included in the subprocessor list maintained at [https://github.com/deslicer/legal/blob/main/privacy/subprocessors.md](https://github.com/deslicer/legal/blob/main/privacy/subprocessors.md).

### 2.4 Consumer Support

**Applicability:** The support programs described in sections 2.1 (Standard Support), 2.2 (Premium Support), and 2.3 (Enterprise Support) apply to enterprise customers governed by the Deslicer General Terms. Individual consumers governed by the Deslicer Consumer Terms receive support through a separate consumer support program.

**Consumer Support Services:**

For individual consumers, Deslicer provides email-based support for consumer subscriptions. Consumer support includes:

- Technical assistance for using consumer-available Offerings;
- Troubleshooting and diagnosis of issues;
- Access to Documentation and knowledge base resources;
- Bug reports and feedback.

**Support Channel:** Email only (support@deslicer.com)

**Response Times:** Deslicer will use commercially reasonable efforts to respond to consumer support requests, typically within 3-5 business days, though response times may vary based on request volume and complexity.

**Limitations:** Consumer support does not include:
- Phone or dedicated support contacts;
- Named Technical Account Managers;
- Priority handling or guaranteed response times;
- Support for enterprise features or configurations;
- Professional Services or custom development.

For detailed information about consumer rights and support, please refer to the Deslicer Consumer Terms.

## 3. Severity Levels

Support requests are classified according to the following severity levels:

### Severity 1 (Critical)
- Production system is down or severely degraded;
- Critical functionality is unavailable;
- Data loss or corruption has occurred;
- Security incident affecting production environment.

### Severity 2 (High)
- Significant functionality is impaired;
- Performance degradation affecting multiple users;
- Workaround is difficult or not available;
- Non-production environment is down.

### Severity 3 (Medium)
- Minor functionality is impaired;
- Issue affects limited number of users;
- Reasonable workaround is available;
- Non-critical configuration or usage questions.

### Severity 4 (Low)
- General questions or requests for information;
- Documentation clarifications;
- Feature requests or Enhancement suggestions;
- Cosmetic issues with no functional impact.

Deslicer reserves the right to re-classify severity levels based on actual impact assessment.

## 4. Customer Responsibilities

To receive support, Customer must:

- Designate authorized contacts for support requests;
- Provide accurate and complete information when submitting support requests;
- Cooperate with Deslicer support personnel in troubleshooting and diagnosis;
- Implement recommended Updates, Enhancements, and configuration changes in a timely manner;
- Maintain supported versions of Offerings and related infrastructure;
- Follow security and operational best practices as described in the Documentation.

Customer is responsible for:

- Maintaining current and accurate contact information;
- Ensuring authorized contacts have appropriate technical knowledge;
- Backing up Customer Content and configurations;
- Implementing changes in test/development environments before production (where applicable).

## 5. Updates and Enhancements

### 5.1 For On-Premises Software

Subject to an active support subscription, Deslicer will make available Updates and Enhancements for On-Premises Software. Updates and Enhancements include:

- Bug fixes and patches;
- Security updates;
- Minor feature improvements;
- Compatibility updates for supported platforms.

Customer is responsible for implementing Updates and Enhancements within Customer's environment.

### 5.2 For SaaS Services

Updates and Enhancements for SaaS Services will be applied by Deslicer in accordance with section 8.3 (Maintenance Windows) of the General Terms. Customer will be notified of significant updates via the service interface or email.

### 5.3 End of Support

Deslicer will provide notice of at least 90 days before discontinuing support for a major version of an Offering. After the end-of-support date, that version will no longer receive Updates, Enhancements, or support services.

## 6. Vulnerability Management

Deslicer will track and assess relevant security vulnerabilities in components Deslicer controls as part of the Offerings. Deslicer will use commercially reasonable efforts to provide remediation for material vulnerabilities through:

- Security patches;
- Maintenance updates;
- Configuration guidance; or
- Workarounds or mitigations.

The timing and method of remediation will be determined by Deslicer based on:

- Severity and exploitability of the vulnerability;
- Availability of vendor patches (for third-party components);
- Technical feasibility of remediation; and
- Available mitigations.

Deslicer does not guarantee that all vulnerabilities will be remediated within specific timeframes unless separately agreed in writing in an Order or Service Level Schedule.

## 7. Exclusions from Support

Support services do not cover:

- Issues caused by Customer's misuse, unauthorized modifications, or failure to follow Documentation;
- Problems caused by third-party software, hardware, or services not provided by Deslicer;
- Issues resulting from Customer's failure to implement Updates or Enhancements;
- Requests for custom development or Professional Services (which are subject to separate Statements of Work);
- Support for unsupported versions or configurations;
- Issues arising from Customer's network, infrastructure, or security configurations outside Deslicer's control;
- Training or consulting services (unless included in a specific Support Program).

## 8. Contacting Support

### 8.1 Support Channels

**Email:** support@deslicer.com

### 8.2 Information to Provide

When submitting a support request, please provide:

- Contact information (name, email, phone);
- Offering name and version;
- Severity level and business impact;
- Detailed description of the issue;
- Steps to reproduce (where applicable);
- Error messages or logs;
- Environment details (for On-Premises Software);
- Any troubleshooting steps already taken.

## 9. Service Level Credits

For SaaS Services, service level commitments and associated credits are set forth in the applicable Service Level Schedule in the Specific Offering Terms. Support response targets set forth in these Support Terms are goals, not guarantees, and do not give rise to service level credits.

## 10. Modifications

Deslicer may modify these Support Terms from time to time. Material changes to Support Programs will be communicated to active customers at least 30 days in advance and will apply to renewals or new Orders after the effective date of the change. Changes will not materially reduce support levels during an active subscription Term.

## 11. Professional Services

Support services do not include Professional Services such as:

- Custom development or integrations;
- Implementation and configuration services beyond standard technical assistance;
- Training and workshops;
- Architecture review and consulting;
- Migration assistance beyond standard guidance.

Professional Services are available under separate Statements of Work in accordance with section 9 of the General Terms.
